WHO WE ARE

Technology should serve people, not the other way around.

FalconAid Tech is a Rwanda-focused technology initiative working on practical tools for first-aid readiness, workplace safety, and community wellbeing.

Explore our mission →
01 / OUR WHY
✳
“A moment of uncertainty should not become a moment without support.”

We want to make guided first-aid assistance easier to access, especially in places where quick action and clear information matter.

OUR MISSION

Help solve delayed first-aid response in Rwanda through technology that connects people with immediate, guided help.

We aim to support safer workplaces and communities while creating opportunities for local skills, innovation, and meaningful work.

CARE+INNOVATION
THE CHALLENGE

In an emergency, knowing what to do can be difficult.

An injury can happen in a workshop, at school, on a construction site, or at home. People nearby may want to help but feel uncertain about the next safe step.

01
◷

Time is important

Confusion about the first steps can slow a person's response when an injury occurs.

02
⌁

Guidance can be unclear

People may not remember first-aid training or know how to find reliable instructions quickly.

03
◎

Preparedness takes planning

Workplaces and institutions need to know where supplies are, what is ready, and what needs attention.

INTRODUCING THE CONCEPT

Meet SmartAid Kit.

A voice-first first-aid assistance concept designed to help people access clear, guided support and encourage better readiness in workplaces and communities.

✓

Voice-first interactionDesigned around spoken prompts and guidance to make interaction more accessible.

✓

Offline-first thinkingThe concept prioritizes core guidance on-device, with local-network reporting planned for supported setups.

✓

Readiness and maintenanceA companion portal concept can help authorized teams track device status, reports, and maintenance needs.
